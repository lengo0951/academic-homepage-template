About

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at Stanford University, advised by Prof. John Smith. My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, numerical optimization, and scientific computing. I am particularly interested in how learned representations encode physical structure, and how that structure can be leveraged for sample-efficient generalization.

Before Stanford I completed my bachelor's at MIT, where I worked with the CSAIL Learning & Inference group on graph neural networks for molecular property prediction. I have spent summers as a research intern at Google DeepMind and Meta AI Research.

Keywords: machine learning · optimization · generative models · scientific ML · representation learning
